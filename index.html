About Adv. Sarfaraz Khan

A decade of dedicated practice in UAE law

I am a Pakistani lawyer and legal consultant with more than 10 years of professional experience in the UAE legal sector. I help individuals, expatriates, investors, business owners and companies understand their rights, assess their position and find the right solution.

Through my association with MAB Advocates & Legal Consultants, I assist with case assessments, document analysis, legal research, drafting of legal memoranda and coordination of legal proceedings.
